## New defect — required micro-fix

**B1 — `onchain_coverage:"error"` is emitted but not in the contract enum.**
`phase-03:138`: "Any analyzer query error → that leg degrades honestly (coverage partial / `onchain_coverage:"error"`)". But `phase-05:35` declares `onchain_coverage` enum `complete|partial|absent` — the `error` value is not contract-declared. Fix either way: (a) extend the enum to `complete|partial|absent|error` + document it, or (b) fold error legs to `partial` — my preference, since `error` means "cannot determine" (closer to partial) while `absent` asserts "no index exists", a different claim. One line either way.
